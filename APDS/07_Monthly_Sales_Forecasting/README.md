# 07 — Monthly Sales Forecasting

Synthetic 12-month sales engine: generation, indicators, quarterly pivots and a 4-panel dashboard.

## Contents
- `utils.py` — `generate_random_sales` (unit-tested).
- `notebook.ipynb` — seeded (42) generation, MoM growth, quarter mapping, quarterly averages AND totals in `output.csv`, printed best month / product / quarter insights, best-month annotated stacked bar, the handout-vs-notebook-vs-vault column alias table, and the three conclusion answers (best product and stability, seasonality, what drives the best month).
- `data/` — generated at runtime by the notebook (initial.csv, final.csv, output.csv) and **git-ignored**.
